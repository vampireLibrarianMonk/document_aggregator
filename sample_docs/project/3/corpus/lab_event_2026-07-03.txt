CLINICAL LABORATORY SAFETY EVENT REPORT

Date: 2026-07-03
System: Automated Chemistry Analyzer Bay 2
Author: R. Delgado, Laboratory Safety Officer

Summary
On 2026-07-03 a reagent spill on the Bay 2 chemistry analyzer contaminated a
sample tray and forced a halt to processing over a three hour window. The spill
was detected at 08:20 local and processing resumed after decontamination at
11:20 local.

Observations
- Reagent-level warning fired at 08:12 local, eight minutes before the spill.
- The affected analyzer was running control-software version 7.1.2.
- Ambient bench temperature was within normal range during the event.
- No patient samples were reported as compromised beyond the affected tray.

Immediate Actions
- Bay 2 analyzer taken offline and decontaminated per protocol.
- Affected reagent lot quarantined pending review.
- Sample tray discarded and re-drawn where required.

Figures
- spill_containment_zone.png: Containment zone around the Bay 2 analyzer.
- sample_tray_layout.png: Sample tray layout showing the contaminated wells.

Preliminary Assessment
The event correlates with a worn reagent seal and a missing secondary catch
tray. Control-software 7.1.2 did not halt aspiration on the reagent-level
warning and is a suspected contributing factor.
