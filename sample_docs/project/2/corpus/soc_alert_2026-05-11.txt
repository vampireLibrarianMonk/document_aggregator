SECURITY OPERATIONS CENTER ALERT SUMMARY

Date: 2026-05-11
System: Customer Portal (web-tier)
Author: A. Nwosu, SOC Analyst

Summary
On 2026-05-11 the SOC detected credential-stuffing traffic against the customer
portal that resulted in 1,240 unauthorized login attempts over a six hour window.
The attack began at 23:05 UTC and was contained after WAF rate-limiting was
enabled at 05:10 UTC the following morning.

Observations
- Anomalous login volume alarm fired at 22:58 UTC, seven minutes before the surge.
- The affected service was running auth-gateway version 3.4.0.
- Source traffic originated from a distributed botnet across 40 countries.
- No successful account takeovers were confirmed during this window.

Immediate Actions
- WAF rate-limiting rule deployed to blunt the attack.
- Affected auth-gateway flagged for version review.
- Impacted customer accounts queued for forced password reset.

Figures
- login_volume_timeline.png: Login attempt volume over the attack window.
- botnet_geo_distribution.png: Geographic distribution of source traffic.

Preliminary Assessment
The event correlates with an exposed legacy login endpoint and missing rate
limiting. Auth-gateway 3.4.0 lacks adaptive throttling and is a suspected
contributing factor.
