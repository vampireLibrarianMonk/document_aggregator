AIRCRAFT MAINTENANCE DISCREPANCY REPORT

Date: 2026-09-06
System: Regional Jet Tail 512 Hydraulic System B
Author: M. Farooqi, Line Maintenance Technician

Summary
On 2026-09-06 Hydraulic System B on tail 512 showed a slow pressure decay that
grounded the aircraft over a seven hour window. The discrepancy was logged at
05:50 local and the aircraft returned to service after a seal replacement at
12:50 local.

Observations
- Hydraulic pressure low advisory triggered at 05:41 local, nine minutes before
  the discrepancy was logged.
- The affected unit was running actuator-controller software B2.3.
- Ambient ramp temperature was within normal range during the inspection.
- No in-flight events were associated with this discrepancy.

Immediate Actions
- Tail 512 grounded and Hydraulic System B isolated.
- Actuator-controller flagged for software review.
- Suspect actuator seal replaced from bonded stores.

Figures
- hydraulic_schematic_system_b.png: Schematic of Hydraulic System B.
- pressure_decay_plot.png: Pressure decay plot over the affected window.

Preliminary Assessment
The event correlates with a degraded actuator seal and delayed leak detection.
Actuator-controller software B2.3 does not annunciate slow decay promptly and is
a suspected contributing factor.
