MANUFACTURING QUALITY DEFECT REPORT

Date: 2026-08-09
System: Injection Molding Line 3
Author: T. Halvorsen, Quality Control Lead

Summary
On 2026-08-09 Line 3 produced housings with a recurring hairline crack defect
that affected 4.2 percent of a production run over a five hour window. The
defect was first flagged at 06:40 local and production was paused after the
root-cause hold at 11:40 local.

Observations
- Cavity pressure deviation alarm fired at 06:31 local, nine minutes before the
  first flagged part.
- The affected line was running mold-controller firmware 2.0.5.
- Melt temperature drifted above the specified band during the run.
- No defective housings were shipped to customers from the held lot.

Immediate Actions
- Line 3 paused and the affected lot placed on quality hold.
- Mold-controller flagged for firmware review.
- Cavity pressure sensor scheduled for recalibration.

Figures
- crack_defect_micrograph.png: Micrograph of the hairline crack defect.
- cavity_pressure_trace.png: Cavity pressure trace over the affected run.

Preliminary Assessment
The event correlates with melt-temperature drift and delayed pressure
compensation. Mold-controller firmware 2.0.5 compensates too slowly for
pressure deviation and is a suspected contributing factor.
