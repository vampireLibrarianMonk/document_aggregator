TELEMETRY GATEWAY TGX-9 FIELD INCIDENT REPORT

Date: 2026-03-02
Site: North Ridge Relay Station
Author: J. Okafor, Field Engineering

Summary
On 2026-03-02 the TGX-9 telemetry gateway at North Ridge dropped 34 percent of
inbound sensor packets over a four hour window. Packet loss began at 02:14 local
and recovered after a manual power cycle at 06:31 local.

Observations
- Buffer occupancy alarm fired at 02:11, three minutes before packet loss began.
- Firmware version 4.2.1 was running on the affected unit.
- Ambient temperature in the equipment cabinet reached 41 C during the event.
- No upstream network faults were recorded by the carrier during this window.

Immediate Actions
- Manual power cycle restored normal throughput.
- Affected unit flagged for firmware review.
- Cabinet cooling fan scheduled for inspection.

Preliminary Assessment
The event correlates with elevated cabinet temperature and a buffer occupancy
alarm. Firmware 4.2.1 buffer management is a suspected contributing factor.
