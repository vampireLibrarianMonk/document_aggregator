NAV BUS INTERFACE SPECIFICATION — SOURCE NOTES

Date: 2026-04-10
System: Navigation Data Bus (Nav Bus)
Author: Interface Working Group

Scope
This document defines the electrical and logical interface between the
Navigation Computer and the Flight Management Unit over the Nav Bus. The Nav Bus
is a serial multiplexed bus operating at a signaling rate of 100 kbps.

Interface Overview
The Nav Bus carries three periodic messages from the Navigation Computer to the
Flight Management Unit. Messages are transmitted on a fixed schedule. The
physical layer uses a differential pair with a nominal bus voltage of 5 volts.

Signal Definitions
- Message POSITION carries latitude and longitude. Word length 32 bits.
  Update rate 20 hertz. Units degrees.
- Message VELOCITY carries ground speed and track angle. Word length 16 bits.
  Update rate 10 hertz. Units knots.
- Message STATUS carries validity and mode flags. Word length 8 bits.
  Update rate 1 hertz. Units discrete.

Data Formats
Numeric fields use two's complement signed integers, most significant bit
first. Timestamps use seconds since power-on.

The signaling rate of 100 kbps was confirmed on the bench. An earlier draft
listed 50 kbps, which is incorrect.
